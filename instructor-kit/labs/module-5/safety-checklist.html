Scratch Safety Checklist

Everything from "Sharing Safely & the Scratch Community" on one page. Tick what you've done, then write your family's sharing rules with your child.

Family: ____________________ Date: ____________
1 · The account (optional!)
  • Decided whether we need an account yet (File → Save to your computer works without one)
  • Username hides real identity: no full name, school, or birth year
  • My email is the parent contact, so I get account notices
  • Password chosen together and kept where we can both find it
2 · Before the first Share
  • I know projects stay private until my child clicks Share
  • We read the Community Guidelines together
  • We had the "before sharing" talk (below)
  • No real name, address, school, phone, or photos in projects or comments
  • Remixes credited in Notes and Credits
3 · Comments & controls
  • We know where the Report button is
  • We know how to turn comments off and delete them
  • I know there's no private messaging: everything is public and visible to me
  • We practiced a kind, curious, specific comment together
4 · Healthy habits
  • Scratch lives on a shared device or in a common room
  • Session length agreed up front, like any screen time
  • More making than browsing: "let's make" over "let's watch"
  • Regular "show me what you made!" check-ins
5 · If an unkind comment appears
  • Stay calm: it's a team job
  • Read it together → Report it → delete it
  • Talk about how it felt; the comment doesn't measure their work
  • Consider turning comments off on that project for a while
6 · By age
  • 5–7: keep it private or family-only; focus on making (ScratchJr has no community at all)
  • 8–11: start sharing with supervision; read comments together at first
  • 12+: more independence, clear expectations: reputation, crediting remixes, not oversharing
The "before sharing" talk

"When we share your project, other kids around the world can see it and leave comments. Most are super nice! But our family rules are: we never share private stuff (our real name, where we live, our school) and if anyone's ever unkind, you show me and we handle it together. Deal?"
