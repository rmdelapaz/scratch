# Module 5 Labs — Teaching Scratch to Your Child (Session S6)

Module 5 is about the *teaching* craft, so there are **no Scratch (.sb3) files** here.
The labs are role-plays, a family safety plan, a ScratchJr build, and a next-project plan.

```
role-play-cards.html   10 kid cards (K1–K10) + 8 scenario cards (S1–S8)
                       + cut-out paper ScratchJr blocks for rooms without tablets
safety-checklist.html  one-page parent checklist for Scratch sharing & community safety
                       (from Lesson 5.2), with a family-rules box to sign at home
```

## Lab map

| Lesson | Lab | Uses | Time |
|--------|-----|------|------|
| 5.1 The Art of Not Taking Over | Hands-off coaching rounds (pairs, swap after 4 min) | Kid cards K1–K6 (demo with K1 Maya) · a buggy project: `../module-3/l3-3-bug-hunt-starter.sb3` or a half-finished Star Catcher | 10 min |
| 5.2 Sharing Safely | Safety checklist + family sharing rules, then a scenario role-play | `safety-checklist.html` (one per family) · kid card K7 Leo · scenario cards S1–S6 | 12 min |
| 5.3 ScratchJr | Build "My Pet Says Hi" on a tablet, then coach a 5-year-old | Tablets with ScratchJr (free) · kid cards K8 Ava, K9 Mia · paper blocks if no tablets | 12 min |
| 5.4 What Comes After Scratch | Name the family's next project (workbook "What Next" page) | Kid card K10 Sam · scenario cards S7–S8 for fast finishers | 5 min |
| S6 Showcase | Children present; kind-comment gallery walk if more than ~10 families | Sticky notes · display laptop · certificates | 25 min |

## Printing & prep

- **role-play-cards.html** — print on card stock, cut on the dashed lines. One set per six
  participants. It prints on 4 pages: kid cards (2), scenario cards (1), paper ScratchJr blocks (1, print in color).
- **safety-checklist.html** — one page, one per family. Print in advance; it's also the take-home
  version of Lesson 5.2.
- Browser print: enable **Background graphics** so the card colors print.
- Kid cards describe a typical behavior, not a script. Remind "children" to play it real, not dramatic.

The coaching look-fors for every lab are in the answer key (`source/module-5/answer-key.html`).
