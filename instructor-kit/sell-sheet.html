Teach Parents to Teach Scratch, Without Building the Course Yourself

A complete, ready-to-run workshop series that takes parents and caregivers with zero coding experience from "I have no idea what this is" to building a real game with their child. Along the way they learn the coaching moves that keep a kid coding at home. Guides, slides, workbooks, answer keys, and Scratch projects are all included.

What You Get

For every module (×5)
  • Facilitator guide: a minute-by-minute session run-sheet, live-demo scripts, and the grown-up-learner stumbles to head off
  • Teach-It role-plays: kid cards and "say this" lines, so parents rehearse coaching before they try it at home
  • Participant workbook: key ideas, block-by-block build steps, a learning journal, and self-checks
  • Answer key: every self-check answered and explained, plus lab solution notes
  • Slide deck: a self-contained projector deck with no software to install
Across the kit
  • 6-session syllabus (2 hours each), which also runs as 3 Saturday half-days, in web and print versions
  • 11 reference .sb3 projects, including a Bug Hunt starter with four planted bugs
  • A Family Build Night plan: kids join Session 5 to build Star Catcher with their parent
  • Participant setup guide plus a celebratory final assessment (project + Teach-It demo) with a rubric
  • Role-play kid cards, a family safety checklist, and a showcase & graduation plan

Why Instructors Choose It

  • Zero prep to teach. Every session has a run-sheet that adds up to 120 minutes, with demos scripted and labs ready. You don't need to be a programmer, only a few steps ahead of the room.
  • Built for nervous grown-ups. It anticipates the fear of "breaking something," trackpad struggles, reading-glasses font sizes, comparing yourself to faster peers, and phones buzzing with family logistics.
  • Two outcomes, not one. Parents learn Scratch and learn to teach it: ask, don't tell; hands off the mouse; praise the process. That's what keeps kids coding after the class ends.
  • The night families remember. Family Build Night and the Session 6 showcase put children at the center. It's the part that fills your next cohort by word of mouth.
  • A free companion course. Every lesson mirrors the free online course Scratch for Parents: Learn It, Then Teach It (rays-scratch.netlify.app), so participants can review, catch up after a missed session, and keep going at home.
