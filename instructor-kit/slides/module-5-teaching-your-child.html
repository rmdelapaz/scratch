5.1

The Art of Not Taking Over

The struggle is the lesson.

Scaffolding · help that fades

Give the least help that unblocks

  • I lead → they lead, I prompt → they do it, I watch → they teach me
  • Too little: "Figure it out." (walks away)
  • Just right: "Which category might have a move block?"
  • Too much: "Drag the blue block here."
WATCH See the fix? Count to five. Ask a question instead.
Ask, don't tell · praise the process

Questions keep them thinking

  • What do you want to happen?
  • What happened instead?
  • Which block is in charge of that?
  • What could we try to test it?
  • "You kept trying different things until it worked."
  • "You tested one block at a time."
  • "You can't make them fall… yet."
GO EASY "You're a genius!" · "See, that was easy!"
Teach-It role-play · how it works

One child, one coach, hands off

  • The "child" draws a kid card and holds the mouse
  • The coach never touches the mouse or trackpad
  • 4 minutes, then swap and draw a new card
  • Trio? The third person watches for questions, praise, "yet"

5.2

Sharing Safely

Private until your child clicks Share.

How the community works

Safer than typical social media

  • Non-profit, minimal data, parent email
  • Nothing public until Share — and an account is optional
  • Moderated, with kid-readable guidelines
  • Report · comments off · delete
  • No private messaging — all visible to you
STILL THE INTERNET Shared room · regular check-ins · "show me any mean comment"
Lab · family plan

Checklist + family sharing rules

  • Tick through the one-page Scratch Safety Checklist
  • Draft 3–4 rules: what never gets shared · where Scratch lives · what to do with a mean comment
  • Draw a scenario card and rehearse the "before sharing" talk

5.3

ScratchJr

Picture blocks for ages 5–7. No reading needed.

Lab · "My Pet Says Hi"

A row, read left to right

👆Start on Tap⤴️Hop 2🔼Grow 2 🎙️My Sound🔽Shrink 2⏹️End
  • Yellow starts · blue moves · purple looks · green is Sound · orange control · red ends
  • Coach: 10–15 min · story first · you read, they tap
WATCH Grow with no Shrink = a pet that gets bigger every tap.
