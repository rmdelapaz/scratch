Instructor-Led Workshop Syllabus

Scratch for Parents: Learn It, Then Teach It

A gentle, hands-on workshop series for parents and caregivers with no coding experience. Over six evenings you learn Scratch yourself, practise teaching it through role-plays, build a real game with your child at Family Build Night, and graduate as their coding buddy.

Format
Instructor-led

6 sessions · 12 hrs
Each session
2 hours · 1 evening
Level
Beginner · no coding

Session Schedule

Times assume a 6:30–8:30 pm evening. Every session opens with a short check-in and journal share, has a 10-minute break, and closes with a journal entry and one thing to try at home this week. Each lesson has a Teach-It pair role-play: one grown-up plays the child from a kid card and the other coaches.

Course Details

Prerequisites

  • None. No coding or “tech” experience needed. Absolute beginners are welcome.
  • Able to open a website and click, drag, and type
  • A laptop or Chromebook, its charger, and a mouse (strongly recommended for drag-and-drop)
  • A current web browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari
  • A child aged about 5–16 to teach is helpful, but not required

What’s Provided

  • A setup guide to finish before Session 1 (about 10 minutes)
  • The participant workbook for all 17 lessons, with a learning journal (yours to keep)
  • Reference Scratch projects for every lab, plus a “rescue file” for Family Build Night
  • Teach-It role-play kid cards, a family safety checklist, and the Game Design Card
  • Free access to the companion online course: rays-scratch.netlify.app

Family Build Night

  • Session 5 is for families. Bring your child (about ages 5–12). No child available? You’ll pair up with another grown-up.
  • Your child is the director and you’re the typist: you code only when asked
  • Session 4 homework: fill in the Game Design Card together, in your child’s words
  • Children come back for the last 25 minutes of Session 6 to present their games and see you graduate

Alternative: 3 Saturdays

  • The same six sessions run as three Saturday half-days (two sessions each, with lunch between)
  • Saturday 1: Sessions 1 + 2 · Getting Started, Core Concepts
  • Saturday 2: Sessions 3 + 4 · Real Projects, Clones & My Blocks
  • Saturday 3: Session 5 (Family Build Night, kids attend) + Session 6, with kids back for the showcase
  • The week between Saturdays 2 and 3 is when families fill in the Game Design Card

What You’ll Be Able to Do

Graduates leave able to build in Scratch and to sit beside their child as a confident coding buddy.

Find your way around the Scratch editor and save your work
Make sprites move, talk, and react with events and sequences
Use loops, conditionals, and sensing to control behavior
Keep score and lives with variables
Build a real game with clones, My Blocks, and broadcasts
Find and fix bugs with a calm 4-step method
Coach your child without taking over the mouse
Set up safe sharing, start ages 5–7 on ScratchJr, and plan what comes next
